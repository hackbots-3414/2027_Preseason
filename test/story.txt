Once upon a time,

Alex went to a Hackbots meeting. He completed the member form, he signed in, and he headed to the programming room. As he stepped through the doorway, however, he noticed that something was off. He looked around, and to his surprise, the whole room was empty! When he blinked, he found himself in a hospital bed, surrounded by nurses and doctors.

Alex had been in a coma for the past twelve years. His reality was an invention of his imagination.

What was real? What was just a hallucination? He would never know for sure.

The end...?
