Every night at exactly midnight, a little golden light appeared at the end of the road. No one knew where it came from. The road had been abandoned for years, with weeds growing through the cracks and trees leaning over it. At the end stood a small wooden gate that nobody remembered building. Twelve-year-old Eli had always been told never to go there, but on the night before his thirteenth birthday, he decided to find out what was beyond it. He grabbed his flashlight, a sandwich, and the brass key his grandfather had left him, then quietly walked toward the mysterious light.

When Eli reached the end of the road, he discovered that the golden light came from a lantern hanging beside the wooden gate. Beneath it was a small note that read, “For the one who finally came.” Eli noticed a tiny lock in the gate and carefully took out his grandfather's brass key. He turned it in the lock, and with a soft click, the gate opened. On the other side was a place that looked like his town, but everything was brighter and more beautiful. The streets were clean, flowers grew everywhere, and people laughed in gardens. Then Eli saw someone standing in the middle of the road. It was his grandfather, who had died three years earlier.

Eli couldn't believe his eyes. His grandfather smiled and said, “You've grown.” With tears in his eyes, Eli asked, “Is this heaven?” His grandfather shook his head and replied, “No. This is where unfinished things wait.” He pointed toward the horizon, where hundreds of golden lanterns floated in the sky. Then he handed Eli the lantern. The moment Eli touched it, he remembered all the things he had left unfinished: a promise he had made to his mother, a letter he had never written, an apology he had been too proud to give, and a dream he had abandoned because he was afraid of failing.

His grandfather looked at him kindly and said, “You don't have to finish everything tonight. You only have to begin.” Eli looked around the beautiful town one last time before stepping back through the gate. Suddenly, the lantern disappeared, and Eli found himself sitting beside the old road in the early morning light. He wondered if everything had been a dream until he reached into his pocket and felt something cold. It was the small brass key. Beneath it was a note written in his grandfather's handwriting. It contained only one word: “Begin.” Eli smiled, stood up, and walked home, ready to make a start.

The end.
